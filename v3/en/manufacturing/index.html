Six Reasons

LEATHER MANUFACTURING OEM
Six Reasons We Are Chosen, Again and Again

Versatility Across Brands

Centered on leather bags and small leather goods, we have built long-term partnerships with more than 15 leading brands in Japan and abroad.

Deep Understanding of Materials

We work with a wide range of materials, including cowhide (calf, nume, embossed), sheepskin, goatskin, deerskin, crocodile, python, horsehide (cordovan), pigskin, and exotic leathers.

Fully Integrated Domestic Production

All products are manufactured in Japan, with zero overseas production. Every process, from cutting to finishing, is carried out in-house at our domestic workshop.

Consistent Quality Standards

We enforce uncompromising quality control on every single piece, maintaining a consistent standard of quality.

Reliable Craftsmanship from Skilled Artisans

Manufacturing is handled by craftsmen with years of domestic production experience, who bring their long-honed skill and sensibility to reliable craftsmanship.

Responsiveness That Sustains Long-Term Relationships

The same craftsman team and a dedicated sales representative provide continuous support, sustaining long-term relationships through attentive customer service.

Equipment

Manufacturing Equipment Supporting Master Craftsmanship

All manufacturing processes are completed domestically at our Sumida-ku workshop. With a multi-skilled team of highly capable craftsmen handling everything from cutting and sewing to finishing, we can meet complex specifications and highly demanding designs.
