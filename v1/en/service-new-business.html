New Business Development & Execution

Turning concepts into revenue-generating businesses.

New businesses don't move forward on the strength of an idea alone. B supply provides end-to-end support: theme exploration, customer problem validation, existing asset inventory, hypothesis building, PoC, early sales and customer acquisition, and business planning. SOLUTIONS

Solutions

New Business Strategy Formulation

Existing Asset Inventory

We take stock of your customer base, technology, brand, channels, and know-how to identify what can be repurposed for new business.

Feasibility Study
