A Message from Our Representative

Japan is home to craftsmanship honed over long years. There are companies rooted in their communities, protecting jobs and building trust with business partners, that have quietly supported society. Yet today, many of them face a shortage of successors.

When a business comes to an end, more than one company disappears. The craftsman's skill, the wisdom accumulated on the ground, relationships with customers, and even the passion built up by founders and employees are lost as well.

We at B supply exist to carry forward such businesses and help them grow even stronger.

We are not a fund that manages money from outside investors.

In 2026, as our first transaction, we succeeded a leather manufacturing workshop in Sumida, Tokyo, that has operated continuously since 1966. Standing in a workshop where craftsmanship has been passed down for 60 years, and witnessing the craftsmen's handiwork firsthand, we came to feel something anew. A truly valuable business cannot be measured by the numbers on a financial statement alone. Within it lies trust built over time, and human pride.

While respecting that value, we build up, one by one, the changes that are needed in management systems, sales strategy, recruiting, digital adoption, and operational improvement. We protect what must be protected and thoroughly change what must change. We take on both with full commitment.

We go to the ground, listen to people's voices, and manage with a hands-on feel. At the same time, we actively adopt technology and new systems. Tradition and innovation are not in conflict. We believe that new capabilities are precisely what is needed to carry a legacy business into the future.

We want to build a society not where businesses built on passion quietly disappear, but where companies of value meet their next steward and continue to grow. By committing to the succession and growth of each company, we aim to carry those businesses into the future and contribute to Japan's development.

Representative Director
