SERVICENEW BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

New Business

Turning concepts into revenue-generating businesses.

New businesses don't move forward on the strength of an idea alone. B supply provides end-to-end support: theme exploration, customer problem validation, existing asset inventory, hypothesis building, PoC, business planning, and early sales and customer acquisition. We handle zero-to-one development as well as new revenue creation built on your existing customer base, technology, brand, and channels.

SOLUTIONS

Solutions

New Business Strategy Formulation

We match market opportunities against your strengths to define which business themes to pursue, and in what order.

Existing Asset Inventory

We take stock of your customer base, technology, brand, channels, and know-how to identify what can be repurposed for new business.

Feasibility Study

We validate market potential, customer needs, profit structure, and feasibility — assembling the evidence needed for a go/no-go decision.

PoC

We test small, validating customer response, value proposition, pricing, and sales approach.

Business Planning

We organize the revenue model, cost structure, KPIs, investment requirements, and execution structure into a plan management can decide on.

Early Sales & Customer Acquisition

We build the customer touchpoints needed in the launch phase and support the activities that lead to first orders and first users.

APPROACH

What Sets Our Support Apart

Not stopping at the concept

We design the business with everything from testing customer response to monetization and sales activity in view.

Starting from existing assets

We leverage the customers, technology, brand, and channels you already have to find highly feasible business opportunities.

Advancing through small-scale validation

The higher the uncertainty, the more we sharpen the business hypothesis through a series of small tests.

Plans you can base investment decisions on

We develop business plans to a level of detail management can actually decide on.
