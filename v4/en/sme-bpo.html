BUSINESS 02 — SMB BPO

We take on the workyou lack the hands for,faster and for less than anyone.

Most small businesses run their daily operations with a small team. Even when you want to focus on your core business, work such as marketing, sales and accounting takes up your time. As operators of a small business ourselves, we have faced the same problem.

In our SMB BPO business, we take on the hands-on work that drives results, as part of your in-house team. We handle everything from setting up the systems to running them day to day.

WHAT WE TAKE ON

We take on the work that creates sales and the work that supports management

We start wherever you are short-handed and take on one area at a time, covering both the work that drives sales and the management work beneath it.

HOW WE WORK

We start small and expand as the numbers come in

We do not take on a large amount of work at the start. Once the way of working is settled in one area, we expand the scope as needed.
