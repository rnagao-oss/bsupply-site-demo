BUSINESS 02 — SME BPO

The work you have no hands for,we take on.

Many small and mid-sized companies run everything from sales to accounting with a handful of people. They want to focus on their core business, but customer acquisition, hiring and admin keep getting in the way. We have heard this again and again in our work.

In our SME BPO business, we take on the practical work needed to grow results, not as an outside vendor but as part of your team. We do not stop at designing a system; we run it day to day.

WHAT WE DO

What we take on

01

Customer acquisition and marketing

We build and run the website, advertising and social media that lead to inquiries and visits.

02

Sales systems and outsourced sales

From building prospect lists to setting meetings and preparing proposals, we carry out sales activity that leads to results.

03

Management accounting and bookkeeping

We organize monthly figures and cash-flow plans so that you have the numbers you need to make decisions.

04

Process improvement and AI

We review routine work and reduce time and effort with AI and cloud tools.

05

Store operations

We review waiting times and work procedures so that stores can run smoothly with fewer people.

06

E-commerce operations

From running your online store to marketplace listings and product page improvements, we handle the work that grows online sales.

HOW WE WORK

How we work

  1. STEP 01

    Listening

    We learn how your work flows today and what the numbers show, and sort out together where hands are short.

  2. STEP 02

    Proposal

    We propose the scope, team, timeline and fees.

  3. STEP 03

    Launch

    We start small and settle how we work and communicate.

  4. STEP 04

    Running and improving

    We share monthly figures, keep improving, and widen the scope as needed.
