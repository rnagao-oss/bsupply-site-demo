BUSINESS 02 — SMB BPO

Most small businesses run their daily operations with a small team. Even when you want to focus on your core business, work such as marketing, sales and accounting takes up your time. As operators of a small business ourselves, we have faced the same problem.

In our SMB BPO business, we take on the hands-on work that drives results, as part of your in-house team. We handle everything from setting up the systems to running them day to day.

FEATURES

Three strengths of B supply's business support

  1. FEATURE 01

    A team built by people who run small businesses

    We run small businesses ourselves and have experienced staffing shortages firsthand. From an owner's perspective, we work with you to decide what to delegate and how to structure the team.

    • One B supply contact person is your single point of contact
    • Our in-house team does the hands-on work
    • We review the scope every month against the numbers
  2. FEATURE 02

    From process design to daily operations

    We do more than perform tasks. We organize procedures, build processes that anyone can follow and then handle day-to-day operations.

    How we build the system

    1. 01Take stock of the current work
    2. 02Write the steps down as a manual
    3. 03Assign owners and tools, then run it
    4. 04Adjust the steps based on monthly numbers
  3. FEATURE 03

    Faster and more affordable with AI

    We use AI and cloud tools to complete routine work faster and with fewer hours, freeing up time and budget for your core business.

    Routine tasks we handle with AI

    • Reading invoices and receipts, and bookkeeping entries
    • Meeting transcription and minutes
    • Building sales lists and drafting emails
    • Web analytics reports

FAQ

FAQ

Do I need to prepare documents before starting?

Anything that shows the current workflow (tools in use, manuals, recent documents) is enough. If there is no manual, we sort it out together in the meeting.

Do I need a work manual to get started?

No. We write the steps down as we take over the work and leave a manual behind. Work that depended on one person becomes something anyone can run.

How are the people assigned?

One B supply contact person is your point of contact, and our in-house team does the work. We combine accounting, HR, sales and web specialists depending on the tasks.

Is there work you cannot take on?

We do not take on work restricted by law to licensed tax accountants, labor consultants and similar professionals. For such work we introduce partner specialists.

Can the scope change along the way?

Yes. We share monthly numbers and widen or narrow the scope as needed.
