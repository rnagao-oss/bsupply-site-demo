Consulting

TOPBUSINESSCONSULTING

B supply joins large companies as part of their team and takes on a wide range of work needed for growth: strategy, new business launches, organization building, sales and marketing, M&A and operational reform. Combining an outside perspective with our own experience running businesses, we work alongside you until decisions are moving on the ground.

OUR ADVANTAGES

Our Strengths

A Practitioner's Perspective

As investors and operators who run our own businesses, we confront real decisions every day. That is precisely why we can offer support grounded in business reality, something theory and frameworks alone cannot capture.

Strategy Built Backward from Execution

What we design is not a strategy that lives and dies on a slide deck. Drawing on experience at both consulting firms and operating companies, we design a working strategy that connects management's decisions with on-the-ground execution.

Support Across a Wide Range of Company Stages

From TSE Prime-listed companies to seed-stage startups and long-established firms over a century old, we support companies regardless of their stage or size.
