FAQ

What types of companies do you support?

We welcome inquiries from companies of any size or industry, from Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime-listed enterprises to seed-stage startups and businesses with more than 100 years of history.

How long does an engagement last?

Engagements can start from one month. We determine the duration based on the challenge.

How are fees determined?

Our fees are generally fixed monthly. We provide a quote based on the scope and team structure.

What team structure do you use?

Each engagement is staffed by one to three team members.

Do you support companies outside major cities or overseas?

Yes.

Can an unprofitable company consult with you?

Yes. We can begin by assessing the current situation together.
