CONSULTING DIVISION

Consulting

We take on the management issues that determine growth, for clients ranging from TSE Prime-listed companies to seed-stage startups and businesses over a century old.

We don't stop at drawing up strategy: we work alongside executives, support their decisions and execution, and stay involved until results appear. You can work from our Osaka headquarters or fully remote, and focus on the work itself wherever you are.

INVESTMENT DIVISION

Business Investment & Succession

We invest B supply's own capital in SMBs with distinctive technology, customer bases, and business assets. The work covers everything from sourcing investment candidates and making investment decisions to post-succession management support and executing growth initiatives.

After investing, we work on the business as principals in its management, not as outside advisors. Depending on your experience and results, you may later join the management of portfolio companies as a director, executive officer, or business lead.
