RECRUITCONSULTING

Director

Lead from the front — build the business and the team.

As Director, you oversee multiple projects and hold final responsibility for delivery quality. While building relationships with client executive teams, you also drive the growth of B supply’s consulting business itself.

Beyond being an excellent player, we are looking for someone who enjoys developing people and building systems. There are plenty of zero-to-one opportunities, from new service development to new business creation.

RESPONSIBILITIES

What You'll Do

  • Oversee multiple projects: issue design and quality control
  • Build relationships with client executive teams and lead proposal work
  • Lead projects across growth strategy, new business, transformation, and organization design
  • Coach and review Business Designers and turn experience into shared knowledge
  • Take part in growing our own businesses, portfolio companies, and succeeded businesses
  • Drive new service development, business creation, and alliance building

PERSON

Who We're Looking For

  • People with project management experience at a consulting firm, or a track record as a business lead
  • Someone who grasps management issues and can lead from issue design through execution
  • Someone who builds trust with client executives and debates candidly
  • Someone who brings out the team's strengths and delivers as a team
  • Someone who takes ownership in building businesses, organizations, and systems

HIGHLIGHTS

Why This Role

Lead entire projects from the upstream

You own the whole arc — issue design, proposals, delivery, and quality — facing executives directly and moving entire projects forward.

Long-term relationships with executive teams

You support the decisions of executives and business leaders and help with growth themes on an ongoing basis — long, deep involvement in a company's change, built on trust.

Work across strategy, organization, and execution

Support spans growth strategy, new business, sales and marketing, operations, and organization design — a role that connects management and the front line.

Develop people and build the team

Through coaching, reviews, and knowledge building you raise the whole team's bar — building the systems that let the organization, not just individuals, deliver value.

Help build B supply itself

Beyond growing the consulting business, you take part in our investment business and business development — helping create B supply's own growth.
