Recruiting Strategy for a Chemical Manufacturer

A long-established chemical manufacturer that could not rely on brand recognition was struggling to hire the mid-career talent needed to drive transformation. B supply provided end-to-end support, from redefining the target candidate profile through to selection operations, building a recruiting system that functions on an ongoing basis.

CHALLENGEChallenge

The client is a long-established chemical manufacturer with a history spanning over a century. While its business foundation was well established, its brand recognition as a company was not a strong asset in the recruiting market. In particular, this brand-recognition wall showed up as sluggish applications in the effort to acquire the mid-career talent needed to drive transformation of its existing business. Without a clearly defined candidate profile, sourcing efforts failed to reach the intended talent pool, a structural issue. The company needed to move beyond ad hoc recruiting efforts and turn them into a system it could sustain.

APPROACHApproach

We first began by redefining the recruiting target: a candidate profile of mid-career talent who could genuinely thrive in driving transformation. Working from selling points that did not depend on brand recognition, we formulated a recruiting strategy built around this candidate profile. Next, we translated the strategy into execution, handling both the design of candidate sourcing and the selection operations on the client's behalf. A defining feature was not separating strategy formulation from execution, with the same team in charge through to the end.

IMPACTImpact

The recruiting strategy, built on redefining the target candidate profile, increased applications from the intended talent pool. By supporting the client through selection operations as well, the recruiting process took hold as a system rather than ending as a one-off initiative. Establishing a recruiting model that does not depend on brand recognition has put the company in a position to keep solving its original challenge of acquiring transformation talent.
