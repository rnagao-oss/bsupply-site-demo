Management Accounting Infrastructure for a Civil Engineering IT Company

We supported a civil engineering IT company whose management systems could not keep pace with the speed of its business growth, so much so that simply grasping the numbers took considerable time. We helped build a management accounting foundation and a KPI dashboard. By making management data visible, we put in place a structure for making management decisions based on the numbers.

CHALLENGEChallenge

The client is a company in the civil engineering IT sector that was in a phase of business expansion. However, the development of its management systems could not keep pace with its rate of growth, and there was no system for quickly retrieving the management figures it needed. As a result, confirming the numbers that underpinned decisions took considerable time. To move business growth to its next stage, the company first needed to establish the management foundation itself.

APPROACHApproach

We supported the client in building a management accounting foundation that allows for the continuous tracking of business figures. We also created a KPI dashboard that lists the indicators needed for day-to-day management decisions in one view. We designed it so that management could discuss decisions based on the numbers. The system is suited to the company's growth stage and built for ongoing practical use.

IMPACTImpact

Management data is now visible, and the environment needed to instantly grasp required figures is in place. Management can now discuss the business based on the numbers. This management accounting system can continue to be used through future phases of business expansion.
