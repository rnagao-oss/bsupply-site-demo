Alliance Strategy for a Femtech Company

This case shows how a seed-stage femtech company overcame a customer acquisition wall through the design of a cross-industry alliance. B supply supported the work from selecting the partner through to executing the initiatives.

CHALLENGEChallenge

The client is a seed-stage company in the femtech space. While it had built awareness and trust in its service, limits were beginning to appear in customer acquisition through marketing run solely in-house. A cross-industry alliance was considered as a way to expand touchpoints with new customers beyond the company's own boundaries.

APPROACHApproach

B supply first organized the structure of the market the client operates in and identified the factors limiting the growth of customer acquisition on its own. Building on this, we identified candidate partners in a highly compatible, different industry and designed a tie-up structure that would create value for both sides. We provided end-to-end support, from building the collaboration scheme with the partner through to execution, aiming to establish a continuous customer acquisition channel rather than a one-off initiative.

IMPACTImpact

A route for acquiring new customers through a partner in a different industry has been established. Selecting the partner with the characteristics of the femtech space in mind has created a sustainable customer acquisition base that does not depend solely on the company's own efforts.
