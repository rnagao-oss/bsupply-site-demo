Organization & Talent Strategy

Hiring is not about adding people — it's about building the structure that moves the business.

Executing business strategy requires the people and organizational structure to carry it. B supply provides end-to-end support, from organizational design and hiring to evaluation and compensation and management systems. Even for companies without brand recognition or scale, we design what roles to hire for, how to hire, and how new hires can perform after joining.

SOLUTIONS

Solutions

Organizational Design

We define the organizational structure, roles, and areas of responsibility your business strategy requires.

Talent Requirement Definition

We organize the required skills, experience, orientation, and expected roles into criteria usable in selection.

Recruiting Strategy

We design target talent profiles, appeal points, channels, and hiring priorities.

Candidate Pipeline Building

Tailored to your hiring targets, we design candidate touchpoints and attraction initiatives.

Selection Process Design

We organize interviews, evaluation items, assessment criteria, and candidate experience, raising the precision of selection.

Evaluation & Compensation Design

We build the evaluation items, compensation design, and operating methods that drive business growth and retention.

Management System Development

So that new hires can perform at their best, we build onboarding, role design, and management systems.

APPROACH

What Sets Our Support Apart

Working backward from business strategy

We first define the organization and talent needed to move the business forward, and then decide headcount and job types from there.

Making requirements concrete

We go beyond "we want to hire good people" — clarifying the role to entrust, the capabilities required, and the criteria for assessment.

Connecting hiring with organization building

We design the structures and management that enable new hires to deliver results after joining.

Hiring that doesn't depend on brand recognition

We clarify the appeal of the business and what makes the role interesting, and decide how to convey them to candidates.
