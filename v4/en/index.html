MESSAGE

MESSAGE

A Message from Our Representative

Carrying the businesses worth preserving into the next era

Japan is home to craftsmanship honed over long years.
There are companies rooted in their communities, protecting jobs and building trust with business partners, that have quietly supported society.
Yet today, many of them face a shortage of successors.

When a business comes to an end, more than one company disappears. The craftsman's skill, the wisdom accumulated on the ground, relationships with customers, and even the passion built up by founders and employees are lost as well.

We at B supply exist to carry forward such businesses and help them grow even stronger.

We are not a fund entrusted with capital from outside investors.
We are a principal investment company that acquires shares with our own capital. That is precisely why we can commit to a business for the long term, unconstrained by any short-term exit deadline.
Our real work begins once we have acquired the shares.

In 2026, as our first transaction, we succeeded a leather manufacturing workshop in Sumida, Tokyo, that has operated continuously since 1966. Standing in a workshop where craftsmanship has been passed down for 60 years, and witnessing the craftsmen's handiwork firsthand, we came to feel something anew.
A truly valuable business cannot be measured by the numbers on a financial statement alone.
Within it lies trust built over time, and human pride.

While respecting that value, we build up, one by one, the changes that are needed: management systems, sales strategy, recruiting, digital adoption, and operational improvement. Protecting what should be protected, and changing what should be changed. We take on both, head-on.

A small team, doing great work.
We go to the ground, listen to people's voices, and manage with a hands-on feel.
At the same time, we actively adopt technology and new systems.
Tradition and innovation are not in conflict.
We believe that new capabilities are precisely what is needed to carry a legacy business into the future.

We want to build a society not where businesses built on passion quietly disappear, but where companies of value meet their next steward and continue to grow.
By committing to the succession and growth of each company, we aim to carry those businesses into the future and contribute to Japan's development.
