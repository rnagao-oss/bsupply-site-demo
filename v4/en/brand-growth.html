BUSINESS 04 — BRAND GROWTH

The brands we inherit,chosen around the world.

The companies we take over often have long histories and real skill, but lack people to run management and marketing.

In our Brand Growth business, B supply steps into the management of the companies it takes over and puts sales, marketing, manufacturing and management in order. We protect each brand's history and its makers' intent while growing it into a brand chosen at home and abroad for years to come.

APPROACH

How we grow a brand

  1. STEP 01

    Understand

    We look at sales, customers, products and production through both numbers and the shop floor, and sort out the issues.

  2. STEP 02

    Plan

    Building on the brand's strengths, we set a multi-year plan and decide what to do in the first year.

  3. STEP 03

    Build

    We put management, sales, marketing and manufacturing systems in place one by one.

  4. STEP 04

    Reach the world

    With a solid base at home, we widen the channels that reach customers overseas.

Our first case: a leather workshop founded in 1966
