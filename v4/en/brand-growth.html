BUSINESS 04 — BRAND GROWTH

We grow inherited craftinto brands chosen around the world.

The companies we take over have long histories and real skill, yet they often lack people to handle management and marketing.

In our Brand Growth business, B supply steps into the management of the companies it has taken over and builds systems for sales, marketing, production and financial management. While protecting each brand's history and its makers' intentions, we grow it into a brand chosen in Japan and abroad for years to come.

FRAMEWORK

We add management systems to the makers' craft

We protect the strengths a company already has. On top of that, B supply takes on four functions that were hard for the company to cover.

APPROACH

We start by understanding where things stand, then expand from Japan to the world

Every company we take over is different, but we work through the same four stages with each one.

CASE 01

Our work has begun at a leather workshop founded in 1966

At the leather workshop in Sumida, Tokyo, that we took over in March 2026, we have started with two of the four functions.
