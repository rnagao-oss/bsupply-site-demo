Sales Strategy for a Semiconductor Company

With a new product launch approaching, we supported a semiconductor manufacturer in building the rationale for its pricing and sales strategy. Pricing design grounded in market research and competitive analysis helped drive a strong start for the new product.

CHALLENGEChallenge

This semiconductor manufacturer was planning to launch a new product. However, it had no pricing rationale grounded in the realities of the market and competition, so its sales teams had to enter negotiations without being able to justify the price. Its sales strategy had not been systematized either. What was needed was a system for translating the new product's value into an appropriate price and bringing it to market.

APPROACHApproach

We designed the pricing based on market research and competitive analysis. Taking into account the positioning of competing products and market acceptance, we clarified the new product's price range and its supporting rationale. We also formulated a launch plan and, going beyond strategy formulation to stay involved through execution, shaped the output into a form the sales team could actually use.

IMPACTImpact

With well-grounded pricing now in place, price explanations in sales negotiations have become standardized. This has accelerated the new product's launch and enabled sales teams to conduct their activities under a consistent policy.
