Business Turnaround Strategy for a Logistics Company

Amid a cash flow crunch combined with a deteriorating revenue structure, B supply provided end-to-end support, from redesigning the business model through to fundraising. Rather than simply keeping the business afloat, we supported the rebuilding of a management foundation for renewed growth.

CHALLENGEChallenge

This seed-stage logistics company, in the process of expanding its business, was experiencing a simultaneous deterioration of its revenue structure and a cash flow crunch. Distortions had emerged in the very mechanism that generated revenue, while its cash flow situation allowed no delay, and the two problems were compounding each other. A one-off fundraising round or partial cost cuts alone would not solve the root cause; the situation called for a review that reached into the structure of the business model itself. Raising funds also required building a proper governance structure. Turning the business around meant moving funding, revenue, and governance forward at the same time.

APPROACHApproach

We first began redesigning the business model itself, which had been eroding the revenue structure. In parallel, to resolve the cash flow crunch, we directly supported the execution of fundraising. Because transparency in the management structure is also essential to earning the trust of capital providers, we worked on building governance as well. A defining feature of this engagement was addressing funding, revenue, and governance together as a single, integrated challenge rather than separately. Because we ourselves are principals who run businesses with our own capital, we provided support attuned to the on-the-ground urgency of a cash flow crunch.

IMPACTImpact

Fundraising was achieved, and the deteriorating revenue structure was rebuilt. We also put governance in place and established a management foundation for renewed growth. By rebuilding funding, revenue, and governance, the company was able to continue operating and move into its next stage of growth.
