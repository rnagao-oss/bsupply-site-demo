Sales Strategy Development for an M&A Advisory Firm

We supported an M&A advisory firm whose deal sourcing depended on individual staff and whose research work consumed so much time that it could not focus on closing deals. By systematizing sourcing and developing a sales strategy, we helped build a structure that lets the team concentrate on closing.

CHALLENGEChallenge

At this firm, deal sourcing depended on the experience and network of specific individuals and had not become reproducible at the organizational level. In addition, researching candidate companies took up a large share of effort, cutting into the time that should have gone to closing. Sales strategy was not systematized either, leaving results highly dependent on individual ability. Growing the business required systems on both the sourcing and sales sides that did not rely on individuals.

APPROACHApproach

We first visualized the deal-sourcing workflow and designed a system to standardize what had been an individual-dependent process. In parallel, we developed a sales strategy grounded in the company's strengths and market environment. We also built a structure to streamline research work and handled the execution of sales activity on the client's behalf. Beyond designing the strategy, we stayed involved through on-the-ground execution.

IMPACTImpact

Standardizing the sourcing process has created a stable flow of meeting opportunities. With the research workload reduced, sales staff can now spend their time on activities that lead to closings. The shift to a system that does not depend on individuals is now a foundation for future growth.
