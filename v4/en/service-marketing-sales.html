SERVICEMARKETING & SALES

Marketing & Sales

Revenue comes from understanding customers and building the means to execute.

When marketing and sales are separated, results become harder to see. Starting from market and customer understanding, B supply designs targets, value propositions, acquisition channels, lead generation, sales processes, pricing, and KPIs as one system. Beyond strategy, we support execution that drives results — early sales, ad operations, and alliance building.

SOLUTIONS

Solutions

Sales Strategy Development

We organize targets, value propositions, deal processes, and sales structures, designing a repeatable path to winning orders.

Marketing Strategy Development

Grounded in customer understanding, we design messaging, channels, content, and acquisition initiatives.

Lead Generation Design

We build the flow from acquisition channels and touchpoint design to nurturing and deal conversion.

Sales Process Design

We visualize the process from first contact to closed deal, turning sales into a repeatable activity.

Pricing

We set evidence-based pricing grounded in market conditions, customer value, competition, and profitability.

Alliance Support

For customer segments hard to reach alone, we build new acquisition channels through partnerships.
