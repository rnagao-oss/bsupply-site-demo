New Business Strategy for a Long-Established Fruit Shop

We supported a fruit shop with a history spanning over a century in its effort to turn existing assets into a new business pillar. We handled the work end-to-end, from auditing the brand, expertise, and customer base through strategy formulation and the creation of sales materials.

CHALLENGEChallenge

With its core business entering a mature phase, this fruit shop, founded over a century ago, believed it needed a new business pillar to drive its next stage of growth. It had assets built up over many years, namely brand strength, product-selection expertise, and a customer base, but no clear internal policy or path for converting them into a new business. It needed to objectively audit the value of its existing assets and shape them into an actual business.

APPROACHApproach

We began by auditing the existing assets: brand, expertise, and customer base. We organized this work from the perspective of how the trust and relationships built up over more than a century of history could be repurposed for the next business pillar. Building on this, we formulated a new business strategy and provided end-to-end support through to creating sales materials that could be used as-is in sales meetings.

IMPACTImpact

Preparations for launching a new business built on existing assets are now complete, and initial sales discussions have begun. Building the intangible strengths of a long-established business, its trust and expertise, into the business strategy has given the company a foothold for driving new business internally.
