Marketing Strategy for a Femtech Company

We supported a femtech company whose ad acquisition efficiency had deteriorated, from reviewing its marketing strategy through to executing ad operations. Through channel redesign, we aimed to restore the company's capacity for growth investment.

CHALLENGEChallenge

At this seed-stage femtech company, customer acquisition through advertising had been driving business growth. However, ad acquisition efficiency had gradually deteriorated, and rising acquisition costs were beginning to squeeze profit. With its existing channel mix, it was becoming difficult to sustain growth commensurate with the ad spend invested. A sense of urgency was growing among management that the very capital available for growth investment could shrink.

APPROACHApproach

We began by scrutinizing the acquisition efficiency of existing ad channels and creative assets and identified the causes of the rise in acquisition costs. Building on this, we overhauled the marketing strategy itself, redesigning channel allocation and messaging. Rather than stopping at strategy formulation, we also took on the execution of ad operations, advancing strategy and on-the-ground operations together. We put in place a structure for continuous support spanning the business plan through to day-to-day operational improvement.

IMPACTImpact

Channel redesign improved ad acquisition efficiency. As a result, the company was freed from cost pressure and gained the capacity to redirect its limited capital to growth investment.
