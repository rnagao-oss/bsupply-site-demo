Handling of Personal Information

B supply Inc. handles personal information provided in connection with job applications as follows.

1. Purpose of use: Your personal information will be used solely for recruitment screening, notifying you of selection results, and responding to inquiries.

2. Third-party disclosure: We will not provide your personal information to third parties except with your consent or as required by law.

3. Security: We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access to, loss of, or leakage of personal information.

4. Disclosure, correction, deletion: We will respond promptly and within reason to requests for disclosure, correction, or deletion.

5. Contact: Please reach us via our contact form.
