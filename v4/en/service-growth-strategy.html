SERVICEGROWTH STRATEGY

Growth strategy

Strategy exists for decision-making and execution.

B supply analyzes markets, customers, competitive dynamics, and profit structures to clarify the growth direction a company should take next. We design mid-term management plans, business strategy, business portfolios, investment decisions, and execution roadmaps as one integrated whole, so that boardroom decisions become frontline actions that move forward through repeated validation and correction.

SOLUTIONS

Solutions

Growth Strategy Development

We identify growth potential and constraints, and organize the initiatives and priorities for the next stage of growth.

Business strategy

We redefine value proposition, customers, profit structure, and competitive advantage to chart a direction for sustained success.

Mid-term plans

We translate management policy into financial plans, investment plans, and execution themes — a plan you can actually make decisions with.

Business Portfolio Design

We map existing businesses, new ventures, and candidates for exit or restructuring, clarifying where management resources should be allocated.

Investment Decision Support

We set decision criteria for investments in business, talent, marketing, and more, so each case is judged consistently.

Business Restructuring & Turnaround

We diagnose issues in profit structure and business operations, and design the business structure and execution plan for renewed growth.

APPROACH

Specific enough for management decisions

Roadmaps that reach execution
