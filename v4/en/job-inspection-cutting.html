RECRUITMANUFACTURING

Inspection & Cutting Staff (Part-time)

Support the atelier's craft from the closest seat.

At our leather atelier in Mukojima, Sumida — founded in 1966 — this job supports the foundations of the craft: inspection, cutting, and preparing materials. No specialist experience is required. What we value most is carrying out each task reliably.

RESPONSIBILITIES

What You'll Do

  • Inspect leather products and parts
  • Assist with cutting, die-cutting, and organizing parts
  • Prepare and sort materials used in production
  • Assist with simple finishing, cleaning, and packing
  • Keep the atelier organized and check inventory

PERSON

Who We're Looking For

  • Someone who handles detailed work carefully
  • Someone who follows set procedures and works accurately
  • Someone interested in craftsmanship and leather goods
  • Someone who works well with the atelier team
  • Beginners welcome, if you approach handwork with enthusiasm

HIGHLIGHTS

Why This Role

Enter the craft with no experience

You can start with inspection and cutting assistance, the foundations of leather making. As you learn the procedures, you can widen your work step by step.

Your care becomes the quality

The precision of inspection and parts preparation determines the quality of the finished product.

Support the whole atelier

Cutting, sorting, preparing materials, keeping things in order — you support the craftsmen and production management staff so they can work smoothly. Not a showy job, but the atelier cannot do without it.

A working style you can shape

As a part-time member you can balance work with life; days and hours are decided in consultation, according to the workload.
