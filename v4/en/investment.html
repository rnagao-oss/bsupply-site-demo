BUSINESS 03 — SMB INVESTMENT

The companies we take over,we hold for good and grow.

Japan has companies with skills refined over decades, rooted in their communities, that have protected jobs and the trust of their customers. Many of them face a shortage of successors. When a business ends, the craft, the know-how on the floor, the customer relationships and the commitment built up over years end with it.

B supply is not a fund managing outside investors' money. We acquire shares and businesses of small and mid-sized companies with our own capital and bank financing, and hold them permanently, never selling. We protect what should be protected and change what must be changed, thoroughly. In management, sales, hiring, digital tools and operations, we build the needed changes one by one and carry the business into the future.
