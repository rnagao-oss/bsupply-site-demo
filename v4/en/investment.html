BUSINESS 03 — SMB INVESTMENT

The companies we take over,we keep for good.

Japan has many small and mid-sized companies with skills refined over decades and customers rooted in their communities. Many of them cannot find a successor.

B supply acquires shares and businesses of small and mid-sized companies using its own capital and bank financing. We keep each company's name, brand, jobs and customer relationships, and we hold every company we take over permanently, never selling it.

OUR POSITION

Another way to carry your company forward

When passing your company to the next generation, selling to a corporation or a fund is not the only option.

OVERVIEW

Investment overview

Target
Manufacturing, retail and construction companies nationwide with revenue of ¥100 million to under ¥5 billion
Management
For each deal, one person from B supply joins the board
Holding period
No exit date; we hold permanently
Funding
Our own capital and bank financing
Support
Through the B4D Program, from finding new demand to creating deals and optimizing operations

B4D PROGRAM

Four forces to grow every company we take over

B supply joins management directly and uses the strengths of the whole group to grow the companies we take over.
