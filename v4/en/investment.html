BUSINESS 03 — SMB INVESTMENT

The companies we take over,we keep for good.

Japan has many small and mid-sized companies with skills refined over decades and customers rooted in their communities. Many of them cannot find a successor.

B supply acquires the shares or businesses of small and mid-sized companies with its own capital. We never sell the companies we take over; we keep them permanently, protecting their skills and jobs and passing them on to the next generation.

OUR POSITION

A different source of capital, and a different holding period

Here is how B supply differs from a typical investment fund, on two points: where the capital comes from and how long the company is held.

AFTER SUCCESSION

After the succession, we keep running the company

B supply steps into the management of every company it takes over. What follows the succession happens in three stages.
