BUSINESS 03 — SMB INVESTMENT

In businesses worth keeping,we invest our own capital.

Japan has many small and mid-sized companies with skills refined over decades and customers rooted in their communities. Many of them, however, cannot find a successor.

B supply is not a fund managing outside investors' money. We acquire shares and businesses with our own capital. We never sell the companies we take over; we hold them permanently, run them with a long-term view, and pass them on to the next generation.

OUR STANCE

Our stance

01

Permanent ownership

Unlike a fund, we do not plan a sale a few years out. Our first priority is that the business lasts.

02

Care for employees and partners

After the succession, we protect the jobs of the people who built the business and the relationships with its partners.

03

We run the business ourselves

From management accounting to sales, hiring and digital, we take responsibility for running the business after the succession.

WHAT WE LOOK FOR

Companies we would like to meet

  • Makers carrying on Japanese tradition, culture and craft
  • Companies with products and brands that customers have supported for years
  • Companies looking for someone to carry the business on
  • Companies that have earned solid trust in their region or industry

PROCESS

From first meeting to succession

  1. STEP 01

    First meeting

    We start by listening to the owner and to what the business means to them. Everything is kept confidential.

  2. STEP 02

    Our offer

    After learning about the business, we present the terms of the succession.

  3. STEP 03

    Due diligence

    We review finances, contracts and people, and settle the final terms.

  4. STEP 04

    Succession and management

    After the succession, we step into management and work on growing the business.

CASE

Taking over a leather workshop in Sumida, Tokyo

In March 2026 we took over a leather workshop in Sumida, Tokyo, in business since 1966. We protect the craft handed down for sixty years while growing the business with sales and digital capabilities.
