RECRUITMANUFACTURING

Production Manager

Organize the flow of manufacturing and bring out the atelier's strength.

What supports leading-brand OEM work is not craftsmanship alone. Production planning, procurement, costing, delivery — the production manager designs the entire flow of manufacturing, so the craftsmen can focus on making.

From spec discussions with brand counterparts to coordination with subcontractors, this is the linchpin connecting the atelier with the outside.

RESPONSIBILITIES

What You'll Do

  • Draw up production plans and manage processes and deadlines
  • Procure leather and materials and manage inventory
  • Coordinate with subcontractors and partner factories
  • Manage costs and improve productivity
  • Align specifications with brand counterparts

PERSON

Who We're Looking For

  • Production-control experience in manufacturing
  • The coordination skills to run multiple projects in parallel

HIGHLIGHTS

Why This Role

Support the quality and flow of the floor

Watching quality, deadlines, processes, and the team's rhythm, you move production forward as a whole — keeping the floor able to make good things steadily, without strain.

Face both quality and productivity

Craft demands both protecting quality and making goods efficiently on schedule. Through process reviews, role design, and better procedures, you build a stronger manufacturing system.

Improve the floor together with the makers

Rather than managing top-down, you listen to the people doing the work and improve in ways that fit the floor — watching both people and process, delivering as a team.

Support craft as a business

By building the system that delivers good goods consistently, you support their value and contribute to the growth of the brand and the business.

Feel improvements land

Process cleanup, standardization, inventory and deadline management — improvements show up quickly on the floor, and small ones accumulate into quality and a better place to work.
