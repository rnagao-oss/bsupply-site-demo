SERVICEOPERATION

Operations

Building the systems that run a business — from management to the front line.

As a business grows, person-dependent work, unclear roles, dysfunctional meetings, messy tool operations, and delayed reporting become constraints on growth. B supply designs and improves operations that actually run in the field — not just management accounting, but the workflows of each function such as sales, customer success, and back office, as well as authority design, meeting structures, and tool operations.

SOLUTIONS

Solutions

Process improvement

We visualize current operations, identify duplication, rework, person-dependency, and bottlenecks, and improve them.

Operations Design

We define workflows, role divisions, areas of responsibility, and decision criteria, building a repeatable operating structure.

KPI Design & Management Accounting

We put in place the metrics and management accounting systems business operations require, enabling improvement grounded in the numbers.

Meeting design

We design the meeting structures and operating rules for decision-making, progress review, and problem-solving.

Authority & Role Design

We make clear who decides what, who executes, and where things get reviewed.

Tool Implementation & Adoption

We select the tools operations require and establish the usage rules that keep them in use in the field.

Corporate Planning & PMO Support

We drive improvement themes, manage progress, and coordinate stakeholders — keeping execution from stalling.

APPROACH

What Sets Our Support Apart

Connecting management and the front line

We translate management policy and KPIs all the way down into the daily work of the front line.

Designing from operational reality

We design based on the actual work, people, tools, and information flows.

Structures and operating rules included

Beyond drawing flowcharts, we design roles, authority, meeting structures, and review methods.

Supporting through to adoption

We don't stop at proposals — we refine through actual operation until the changes take root in the field.
