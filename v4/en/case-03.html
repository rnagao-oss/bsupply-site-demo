Generative AI Market Research for a Major Advertising Agency

For a major advertising agency, we supported everything from mapping the structure of the generative AI x marketing market and inventorying use cases to formulating market entry initiatives.

CHALLENGEChallenge

The client could not fully grasp the overall impact that the spread of generative AI would have on its marketing business. Given the rapid pace of technological change, it lacked sufficient internal grounds for judging which areas to enter and which to defer, and had no solid basis for discussions on new service development and company-wide AI strategy.

APPROACHApproach

Focusing on the generative AI x marketing market, we first surveyed the market as a whole, then organized use cases and narrowed down realistic entry opportunities for the client. We supported the process end-to-end, from research through insight extraction to initiative planning, shaping the output into a form that could withstand scrutiny at management meetings.

IMPACTImpact

Priority focus areas were identified, leading to the development of an initial roadmap for new service development. The research findings are also being used as a foundation for company-wide discussions on AI strategy.
