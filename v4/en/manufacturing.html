BUSINESS 05 — LEATHER OEM

B supply has succeeded a leather manufacturing workshop founded in 1966. By combining 60 years of craftsmanship with our sales capability and digital infrastructure, we are growing this neighborhood workshop into a brand that can compete worldwide.

In 1966, a leather workshop began in the shitamachi district of Tokyo.

For 60 years, its craftspeople have preserved the skills by hand.

We will carry those skills and that commitment

on to the next era.

SIX REASONS

── LEATHER MANUFACTURING OEM ──Six Reasons We Are Chosen, Again and Again

01

Versatility Across Brands

Centered on leather bags and small leather goods, we have built long-term partnerships with 20 brands in Japan.

02

Deep Understanding of Materials

We work with cowhide (calf, nume, embossed), sheepskin, goatskin, deerskin, horsehide (cordovan), and pigskin, as well as exotic leathers such as crocodile and python.

03

Fully Integrated Domestic Production

All products are manufactured in Japan, with zero overseas production. Every process, from cutting to finishing, is carried out in-house at our domestic workshop.

04

Consistent Quality Standards

We check the quality of every piece to keep the finish consistent.

05

Reliable Craftsmanship from Skilled Artisans

Manufacturing is handled by craftsmen who built their experience in Japan, bringing long-honed skill and sensibility to reliable craftsmanship.

06

A Structure That Sustains Long-Term Relationships

A dedicated sales representative stays your point of contact, and the same team of craftsmen handles manufacturing. We handle every order with care so that our relationships last.

EQUIPMENT

Manufacturing Equipment Supporting Master Craftsmanship

All manufacturing processes are completed at our workshop in Sumida-ku. Because our craftsmen are multi-skilled across cutting, sewing, and finishing, we can handle complex specifications and demanding designs.
