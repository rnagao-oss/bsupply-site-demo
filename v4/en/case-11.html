New Business Strategy for a DX Support Company

A DX support company was searching for a new revenue pillar. We supported the launch of an education business from the ground up.

CHALLENGEChallenge

In addition to its existing business, the company had conceived of launching an education business as a new revenue pillar. However, it lacked sufficient in-house know-how for building a business from scratch, and progress had stalled even at the stage of outlining the business. It needed to advance service design and marketing simultaneously, but with limited resources, it could not determine where to start. The challenge was to keep the concept from remaining purely theoretical and instead shape it into a service that could actually launch.

APPROACHApproach

We provided end-to-end support for launching the education business, from concept through execution. Alongside the business design, we also handled the marketing work, advancing planning and customer acquisition at the same time. Working backward from the goal of launching the service, we organized the necessary decisions and practical tasks in sequence and, rather than stopping at advice, worked on the ground as a party to execution.

IMPACTImpact

By staying engaged through to the service launch, the education business, which had remained at the concept stage, actually got off the ground. A mechanism for initial customer acquisition was also put in place, giving the business a foundation to run on its own.
