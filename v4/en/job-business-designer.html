RECRUITCONSULTING

Business Designer

Design the strategy — and see it through to results.

Consulting at B supply is not about delivering a report and moving on. Our job is to move management decisions and execution forward for a wide range of clients, from TSE Prime-listed companies to seed-stage startups and century-old businesses.

As a Business Designer, you build hypotheses on clients’ management issues, analyze them, debate directly with executives, and stay alongside execution until results materialize. You can work from our Osaka HQ or fully remote, focusing on your work wherever you are.

RESPONSIBILITIES

What You'll Do

  • Drive growth-strategy, new-business, and business-transformation projects for client companies
  • Build business hypotheses grounded in market, customer, and competitor research
  • Design business plans, KPIs, and execution roadmaps
  • Discuss with executives and business leads, structure the issues, and prepare materials
  • Manage project progress, coordinate stakeholders, and support execution of initiatives
  • Hands-on support for whatever growth requires — sales, marketing, operational improvement, and more

PERSON

Who We're Looking For

  • People with planning or business-driving experience at a consulting firm, operating company, or startup
  • Someone who can structure problems into issues, hypotheses, and actions
  • Someone who goes beyond documents and moves execution and improvement forward themselves
  • Someone who engages with executives and business leads sincerely and candidly
  • Someone who enjoys fast-changing environments and keeps learning while moving forward

HIGHLIGHTS

Why This Role

Work close to executives on the themes that matter

This role deals with themes close to management — growth strategy, new business, and transformation. You debate directly with executives and business leads, structure the issues, and shape the moves that push the business forward.

Not just strategy — execution too

You stay involved through execution, validation, and improvement — seeing how the strategy actually behaves in the field and how it turns into results.

A wide range of business themes

From growth strategy and new business to marketing, sales, operations, and organization building — you work across whatever it takes to grow a business, not within one narrow specialty.

Sharpen both logic and execution

You develop both the ability to analyze markets and customers, form hypotheses, and turn them into plans and roadmaps, and the ability to bring people along and actually move things forward.

Grow as a principal in business building

B supply doesn't only consult — we also invest in and run businesses ourselves. You build experience as a principal who creates and operates businesses, not just as an outside advisor.
