SERVICEM&A STRATEGY

M&A Strategy

Working backward from growth, not from the deal.

M&A is a means of realizing growth strategy. B supply provides end-to-end support: clarifying acquisition and alliance objectives, defining target domains, sourcing strategy, business due diligence, investment decisions, and PMI. We validate the market potential, profit structure, synergies, and execution risks of target companies, and distill them into criteria management can act on.

SOLUTIONS

Solutions

M&A Strategy Development

We clarify the objectives of acquisitions and alliances, defining target domains from the perspective of the whole business portfolio.

Sourcing Strategy & Infrastructure

We put in place the research methods, evaluation criteria, and management structures for sourcing candidate companies continuously.

Business Due Diligence

We validate market potential, competitive landscape, profit structure, growth headroom, and synergies.

Investment Decision Support

We distill findings into investment criteria, organized so the board can make the decision.

PMI

We design the post-acquisition integration policy, structure, KPIs, and execution plan, and support you through execution.

Business Succession, JV & Alliances

We design paths to growth — including succession, joint ventures, and alliances — not just acquisition.

APPROACH

What Sets Our Support Apart

Working backward from growth strategy

Never M&A for its own sake — we clarify which growth challenge the deal is meant to solve.

Criteria you can decide with

We document the verified facts as a basis for decisions that can be used in management meetings.

Making sourcing systematic

Not a one-off candidate search — we enable you to keep finding candidate companies on your own.

Looking ahead to post-acquisition results
