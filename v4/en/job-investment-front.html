RECRUITINVESTMENT

Investment Front Member

See one company through — from the first meeting to its full potential.

We invest B supply's own capital in small and medium-sized companies with distinctive technology, customer bases, and business assets. From sourcing to the investment decision, post-succession management support, and growth execution — this is the work of creating a business's next possibility.

After investing, we face the business as principals of its management, not as outside advisors. Depending on your experience and results, there are opportunities to join portfolio companies as a director, executive officer, or business lead.

RESPONSIBILITIES

What You'll Do

  • Source investment candidates, run first meetings, and build relationships with owner-managers
  • Research and analyze markets, industries, competitors, and financials
  • Structure investment hypotheses, growth hypotheses, and risk issues
  • Prepare investment memos, business plans, and valuations
  • Drive due diligence, contracts, and closing
  • Support management post-investment and design and execute value-up initiatives
  • Hands-on support for portfolio growth — sales, hiring, digital, operational improvement, and more
  • Join portfolio companies as a member of management, depending on the situation and your results

PERSON

Who We're Looking For

  • People with experience in banking, a sales-driven company, a trading company, a consulting firm, or a planning / sales / marketing role at an operating company
  • Strong interest in SME investment, business succession, and owner-managed companies
  • Someone who understands a business through its people and front line, not numbers alone
  • Someone who goes beyond analysis and moves the business forward hands-on
  • Someone who faces owners sincerely and builds long-term trust

WELCOME

Nice to Have

  • Hands-on experience in M&A, business succession, investment evaluation, due diligence, or PMI
  • Execution experience directly tied to growth — sales, marketing, hiring, or process improvement
  • Experience as a business lead, or in corporate planning, new business development, or subsidiary management

HIGHLIGHTS

Why This Role

From the deal's front door to the shop floor

You engage across the whole process — sourcing, building relationships with owners, the investment decision, and post-investment growth support. You judge a business's potential and think through how to grow it after the investment.

Discover the potential of SMEs yourself

Our targets are SMEs with distinctive technology, customer bases, and business assets. You find strengths the market hasn't fully priced in and shape them into investment opportunities for the next stage of growth.

Judge businesses through numbers and the field

In addition to financials and market data, you understand a business through the owner's thinking, its operations, and its customer relationships — visiting in person and improving the accuracy of each investment decision.

Own the value-up after investing

Investment is not the end: you help execute what growth requires — sales, hiring, digital, operational improvement — getting deeply involved as a principal in raising business value.

A path to running a portfolio company

Depending on your experience, aptitude, and results, and the portfolio company's situation, you will have the opportunity to join its management as a director, executive officer, or business lead. Front-line investment experience becomes the starting point of a management career.
