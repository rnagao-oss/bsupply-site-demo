Consulting

We support mid-term business planning, new business launches, and building AI-native business foundations and organizations. Drawing on our experience in management at consulting firms and major corporations, we stay involved until the plan is moving on the ground.
02SME BPO

We take on the work there is no time for.

SME BPO

From building customer acquisition and sales systems to handling accounting and back-office work and streamlining operations with AI, we carry the hands-on work small and mid-sized companies need to grow.
03SME Investment

We invest our own capital in Japan's craft and tradition.

SME Investment

We invest our own capital in small and mid-sized companies with distinctive technology, tradition and customer bases. With no deadline to sell, we work with each business on the premise of passing it on to the next generation.
04Brand Growth

We grow the businesses we take over into brands chosen worldwide.

Brand Growth

We step into the management of the companies we take over and rebuild their sales, marketing and manufacturing with our own growth program. Our first case is a leather workshop in Sumida, Tokyo, founded in 1966 and taken over in March 2026.
05Leather OEM

Sixty years of craft behind your brand.

Leather OEM

At our own workshop in Sumida, Tokyo, we manufacture leather bags and small goods for other brands, from small-lot samples to mass production, entirely in Japan.
